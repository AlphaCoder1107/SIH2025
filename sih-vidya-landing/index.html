FAQ

Who can participate?

All eligible Vidya University students can participate as per SIH guidelines. Form teams as per rules and consult your department coordinator if unsure.

What are the key dates?

Dates will be announced soon. Keep an eye on the Updates section for official announcements.

How do we register our team?

Use the Registration Form in Resources for internal submission. The SIH Official Registration Form will be listed when available.

What should the proposal include?

Follow the Submission Guide (Resources) for required sections: problem statement, solution approach, tech stack, team roles, and timelines.

How will teams be evaluated internally?

Evaluation will consider problem-fit, innovation, feasibility, demo readiness, and presentation quality. Details will be shared in updates.

Where can I ask questions?

Contact the SIH coordination cell at +91-8077310594 or reach your department coordinator.
